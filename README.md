# business-category-bubble-chart-tableau

Your 5 insights can be based on the actual dashboard

Technology has the highest total sales among the three categories, followed by Furniture and Office Supplies.
Technology also has the highest total profit, while Furniture has the lowest profit among the three categories.
In the Bubble Chart, Phones, Chairs, Storage, and Binders have relatively large bubbles, indicating higher quantities compared with smaller sub-categories.
The Sales vs Profit scatter plot shows that some sub-categories generate high sales and high profit, while a few sub-categories have negative profit despite generating sales.
The Region filter allows users to compare business performance across Central, East, South, and West regions interactively.

2 recommendations
Focus on high-performing Technology sub-categories by maintaining adequate inventory and identifying opportunities to increase sales further.
Review sub-categories with negative or relatively low profit, especially where sales are significant, and investigate pricing, discounts, and operating costs.
